About us

Giving your home a new & luxurious style

Established on 2 January 2000, Mumtaz Tile Zone & Sanitary Ware is one of the leading tile and sanitary ware showrooms in Khairpur Mirs. We supply premium-quality tiles, sanitary products and modern home-improvement solutions at competitive prices, including selected products imported from China.
