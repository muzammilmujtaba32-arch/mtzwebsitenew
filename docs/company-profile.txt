Mumtaz Tile Zone & Sanitary Ware
Company Overview Mumtaz Tile Zone & Sanitary Ware is one of the leading tile and sanitary ware showrooms located in Khairpur Mirs, Sindh, Pakistan. Established on 2 January 2000, the company provides premium-quality tiles, sanitary products, and modern home improvement solutions at competitive prices. The company imports selected products from China and is committed to quality, trust, and customer satisfaction.
